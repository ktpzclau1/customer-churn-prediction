# Customer Churn — Exploratory Data Analysis Findings

## Project Objective

The objective of this analysis is to understand customer churn patterns and identify features that may help predict which customers are likely to leave a telecommunications company.

## Key Findings

1. **Overall Customer Churn:** Approximately 26.5% of customers in the dataset left the company.
2. **Contract Type:** Customers with month-to-month contracts have a considerably higher churn rate than customers with one-year or two-year contracts.
3. **Monthly Charges:** Customers who left had higher average monthly charges than customers who stayed.
4. **Customer Tenure:** Customers who left had shorter average tenure, suggesting that churn is more common among relatively newer customers.

## Conclusion

Contract type, monthly charges, and customer tenure are promising features for the customer churn prediction model. Further data cleaning and model evaluation are required before drawing predictive conclusions.

These findings describe associations in the dataset and do not establish causation.
